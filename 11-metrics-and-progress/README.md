# Metrics and Progress

Use this file to record actual progress, current baselines and lessons. If a metric has not been measured, write `Not measured` rather than estimating.

## Measurement principles

- Choose metrics to support decisions, not to decorate a report.
- Keep a baseline and measurement period for comparisons.
- Record the platform, content type and sample size.
- Separate observed metrics from interpretations.
- Do not claim causation from a simple before-and-after comparison.
- Keep private personal or audience information out of public reporting.

## Monthly review

- What was published?
- Which audience questions appeared repeatedly?
- Which content was useful, and what evidence supports that view?
- What did not work as expected?
- What changed in the strategy?
- What should be stopped, continued or tested next?
