# Content Strategy

## Strategic goal

Build a dependable library of useful, understandable and responsible financial education content. Optimise for comprehension, trust and sustained usefulness, not just reach.

## Initial content pillars

1. **Money basics:** budgeting, emergency funds, saving habits and everyday decisions.
2. **Credit and borrowing:** credit scores, credit cards, interest, fees, EMIs and repayment concepts.
3. **Investing foundations:** risk, diversification, compounding, time horizons and common terms.
4. **Digital finance and safety:** scams, fraud signals, privacy, payment safety and verification habits.
5. **FinTech explained:** how products, companies and business models work, with conflicts and incentives made clear.

Prioritise pillars after audience research and based on the capacity to fact-check content.

## Format roles

- **Short videos:** one question, one explanation, one useful action or question to investigate.
- **Long videos:** structured explanations with examples, sources, limitations and recaps.
- **Carousels and posts:** referenceable step-by-step explainers and terminology.
- **Written resources:** evergreen guides, checklists and deeper explanations.
- **StockRani Decodes:** a named series for company, product and fintech business-model explanations.

## Editorial workflow

1. Capture an audience question.
2. Check relevance and source quality.
3. Write a precise claim and learning outcome.
4. Research and cite primary or credible sources.
5. Script in plain language and label illustrative examples.
6. Review factual accuracy, risks, outdated information and disclosures.
7. Produce and publish with a useful title and accessible captions.
8. Record performance and audience questions.
9. Review what was learned and update content when facts change.

## Quality checklist

- [ ] Clear audience and question
- [ ] One primary learning outcome
- [ ] Credible sources checked
- [ ] Rates, rules and figures date-checked
- [ ] Risk and limitations included
- [ ] No promise of returns or personal recommendation
- [ ] Captions and language are understandable
- [ ] Paid relationship or affiliate link disclosed
- [ ] Publish date and review date recorded

## Frequency

Set a publishing cadence only after considering available time for research, production, review and rest. Consistency must not come at the cost of accuracy.
