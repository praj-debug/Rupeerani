# Founder Story

## Why this section exists

This is the living record of how RupeeRani began, why Prajakta Mane chose to build it and how the idea changes through actual work. Tell the story candidly without forcing it into a polished success narrative before the evidence exists.

## Founder

Prajakta Mane is a FinTech Product Operations and Strategic Partnerships professional. Work around lending, financial products, customer journeys and operations has shaped her interest in making money concepts more accessible.

## The original problem

Starting hypothesis: many young adults encounter decisions about their first salary, budgeting, credit cards, EMIs, education loans, saving and investing before they feel confident understanding the terms, trade-offs and risks.

This is a hypothesis to validate with audience conversations and evidence, not a claim that every young adult has the same need.

## Why build RupeeRani

The ambition is to create a clear, practical and trustworthy home for financial education. The brand should help people ask better questions, understand options and recognise when they need advice specific to their circumstances.
