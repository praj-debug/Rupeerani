# Founder Story

## Why RupeeRani Started

RupeeRani grew out of my professional journey and a question I kept coming back to: **Why are people expected to make important financial decisions before they fully understand how money works?**

Through my work across financial services, lending and education loans, I saw how much students had to navigate when financing their education. Understanding a loan, its interest rate, repayment obligations and long-term implications can be difficult, especially when the fundamentals of personal finance are still unfamiliar.

At the same time, financial content was everywhere, across YouTube, Instagram and other platforms. Yet I felt there was room for something different: explanations that go beyond isolated tips and trending topics to explore the basics, the reasoning behind financial decisions and how different parts of the financial world connect.

## From StockRani to RupeeRani

I started StockRani in August 2022 with a focus on stock-market education. As my own understanding of finance and the needs people encounter grew, I began to see that financial literacy could not stop at the stock market.

People also need to understand income, budgeting, credit, loans, saving, investing and the trade-offs behind everyday money decisions. They need context, not just information. They need to understand not only what a financial product is, but why it exists, how it works, what it costs and what to consider before making a decision.

My interests also expanded into careers, professional growth, companies and the business models shaping the world around us. That led me to think beyond one content niche and build a broader learning ecosystem.

**RupeeRani emerged from that evolution.**

RupeeRani is the umbrella brand for this broader vision:

- **StockRani:** Financial literacy and investing education.
- **StockRani Decodes:** Explanations of financial concepts, products, companies and fintech business models.
- **StockRani Studio:** A planned space for career and professional resources.

The goal is not simply to add another channel to a crowded internet. It is to create useful, connected learning that helps people move beyond knowing *what* something is to understanding *why* it matters, *how* it works and *what* they should consider next.

RupeeRani is my attempt to build that, one explanation, experiment and lesson at a time.

## What I Have Learned So Far

This story reflects my experience and the questions that motivated the project. The broader audience need still has to be tested through conversations, research and evidence. The brand should stay open to learning and changing its approach as that evidence grows.
