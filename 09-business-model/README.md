# Business Model and Future Sustainability

## Principle

Build a trustworthy educational product first. Consider revenue models only where they serve the audience, are disclosed clearly and do not distort content decisions.

## Current status

No current RupeeRani revenue, profitability or validated monetisation is claimed in this repository. The options below are areas to research and test, not existing income streams.

## Potential future models

- Paid guides, templates or learning resources that provide clear additional value.
- Workshops or educational sessions with transparent scope.
- Clearly disclosed brand partnerships that fit the audience and editorial standards.
- Carefully selected affiliate relationships with disclosure and independent evaluation.
- Membership or a structured learning product if sustained audience demand is demonstrated.

Any financial product partnership needs conflict-of-interest controls and careful legal and regulatory review. Never sell audience trust for short-term income.

## Evaluation criteria

Score each option on audience usefulness, trust risk, required effort, likely costs, repeatability, compliance needs and evidence of willingness to pay.
