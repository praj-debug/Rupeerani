# Collaborations, media and partnership enquiries

RupeeRani is an early-stage financial education initiative focused on making money concepts more accessible to young Indians. We are interested in exploring partnerships that help people learn, ask better questions and make more informed financial decisions.

## Potential collaboration partners

| Partner type | Possible collaboration | What a partner can contribute |
|---|---|---|
| Colleges and student communities | Financial-literacy sessions, student Q&As, beginner-friendly guides | Access to student questions and community distribution |
| Fintech and consumer-finance companies | Educational explainers, product-literacy sessions, co-created FAQs | Subject-matter expertise and responsible access to product information |
| Financial educators and creators | Co-authored explainers, interviews, livestreams, content swaps | Complementary expertise and relevant audiences |
| Employers and early-career communities | Salary, budgeting, credit and benefits workshops | Access to young professionals and real learning needs |
| NGOs and financial-inclusion groups | Workshops and accessible-language resources | Community trust, local context and programme experience |
| Media, podcasts and event organisers | Founder interviews, guest articles, panel discussions | Distribution and opportunities to share practical lessons |

These are target categories, not claims of existing partnerships.

## Formats to explore

- Short-form educational content and co-created explainers
- Guest posts, newsletters and interviews
- Online webinars and college/community workshops
- Beginner-friendly downloadable resources
- Research-led discussions about common financial-literacy questions
- Carefully scoped sponsored education, with clear disclosures and editorial safeguards

## Editorial and trust principles

- Education must remain useful even when a partner is involved.
- Disclose sponsorships, affiliate arrangements and other material relationships clearly.
- Never promise returns, imply guaranteed outcomes or disguise advertising as independent advice.
- Verify financial statements and product terms against current primary sources.
- Do not share audience or user data with partners without a clear lawful basis and appropriate consent.
- Decline partnerships that require misleading claims or compromise reader trust.
