Privacy Policy

RupeeRani and StockRani respect your privacy. This policy explains what information is collected when you subscribe to product updates and how it is used.

Information we collect

When you submit the product-update signup form, we collect your email address and the signup date. The subscriber list may also record the source of signup, consent status and subscription status.

How we use your information

We use your email address to send the free EMI Tracker resource, announce new trackers or calculators, and share meaningful updates about StockRani tools. We do not use the list for unrelated daily promotions.

Where information is stored

Signup information is stored in a Google Sheet managed by RupeeRani using Google Apps Script. Google may process information under its own terms and privacy practices.

Sharing and sale

We do not sell subscriber email addresses. We do not share them with third parties for their independent marketing. Access is limited to people who need it to manage the subscriber list and communications.

Retention and your choices

We retain your email while you remain subscribed. To unsubscribe, request access, correction or deletion of your email address, contact connect.rupeerani@gmail.com with the subject “Privacy request”. We will process reasonable requests and update the subscriber list.

Security

We take reasonable steps to restrict access to the subscriber sheet and associated tools. No online service or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

Children

This website and its product updates are intended for a general audience and are not knowingly directed at children under 18.

Changes

We may update this policy when our services or practices change. The latest version will be published on this page with its updated date.

Contact

Questions or privacy requests: connect.rupeerani@gmail.com.
