# Brand Identity and Messaging

Document the verbal and visual system that makes RupeeRani recognisable and consistent. Keep decisions, explorations and final approvals distinct.

## Identity components

- Brand name and spelling
- Logo and safe-space rules
- Colour palette and accessibility contrast
- Typography and layout principles
- Image and illustration style
- Tone of voice and vocabulary
- Messaging hierarchy and calls to action

## Tone of voice

**Clear, grounded, warm, curious and responsible.** Explain rather than lecture. Be friendly without pretending finance is easy in every circumstance. Use examples but disclose when numbers are illustrative.

## Writing principles

- Define technical terms the first time they appear.
- Separate facts, estimates, opinions and examples.
- Use current and credible sources for time-sensitive claims.
- Explain risks and trade-offs, not only benefits.
- Avoid shaming, fear, urgency tricks and guaranteed-result language.
- Write in the language most useful to the audience, with translations reviewed for meaning.

## Messaging framework

- **One-line description:** RupeeRani makes everyday money concepts easier to understand.
- **Vision:** a financially informed generation able to make decisions with clarity, confidence and independence.
- **Mission:** explain personal finance, credit, saving and investing in simple, practical and responsible language.
- **Proof:** build this section using verified published work, transparent sourcing and demonstrated learning. Do not add unverified traction.
