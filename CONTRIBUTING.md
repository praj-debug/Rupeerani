# Working on RupeeRani

This repository is the public record of RupeeRani's development. Changes should improve clarity, usefulness, accuracy or transparency.

## Documentation standards

- Use plain language and descriptive headings.
- Label assumptions, plans and unvalidated hypotheses.
- Cite credible sources for financial facts, rules, rates and other time-sensitive claims.
- Add dates to milestones and measurements.
- Avoid personal data and confidential information.
- Do not publish exaggerated claims about audience size, revenue, impact or product readiness.

## Content standards

Financial education content should explain risks and limitations, avoid guarantees, and distinguish general information from personalised advice. Disclose sponsorships, affiliate links and conflicts clearly.

## Status labels

- **Idea:** not yet assessed.
- **Planned:** chosen for future work but not completed.
- **In progress:** work has started.
- **Live:** available to the intended audience.
- **Measured:** outcome captured with a source and period.
- **Paused / retired:** no longer active, with a note explaining why.
