## Architecture

RupeeRani is the umbrella brand and intended main website for the financial education ecosystem. StockRani is a brand within RupeeRani, not an independent website.

## Rules for sub-brand decisions

- Every property needs a distinct audience need and clear role.
- Avoid duplicate content identities without a meaningful distinction.
- Shared values, disclosure standards and visual consistency apply across all properties.
- Do not imply a separate website, launched product or revenue stream unless it exists.
- Do not launch a sub-brand simply because a name exists. Validate its purpose and operating capacity first.
- Keep logo files in `assets/logos/` and use relative links so they render inside this repository.
