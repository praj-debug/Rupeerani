# RupeeRani Brand Family

## Architecture

RupeeRani is the umbrella brand and intended main website for the financial education ecosystem. StockRani is a brand within RupeeRani, not an independent website.

### RupeeRani

Role: umbrella identity, shared standards, home for the overall mission and future information architecture.

### StockRani

Role: financial literacy and investing education content. The focus is beginner-friendly explanation, risk awareness and practical money knowledge. It is not an education-loan sales or refinancing business.

### StockRani Decodes

Role: a named explanatory series for financial concepts, products, companies and fintech business models. It should explain how something works, who it serves, what incentives exist and what users should check.

### StockRani Studio

Role: a planned space for career and professional resources. Keep it clearly scoped and do not let it confuse the core financial education promise. Validate audience need before investing in a separate product experience.

## Rules for sub-brand decisions

- Every property needs a distinct audience need and clear role.
- Avoid duplicate content identities without a meaningful distinction.
- Shared values, disclosure standards and visual consistency apply across all properties.
- Do not imply a separate website, launched product or revenue stream unless it exists.
- Review the architecture when audience evidence or execution capacity changes.
