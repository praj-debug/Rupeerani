# Website and Product Roadmap

## Intended role

RupeeRani is the umbrella brand and intended main website. The website should help visitors quickly understand the mission, find reliable explanations and navigate to the content most relevant to their questions.

## Experience principles

- Mobile-first and fast-loading.
- Clear navigation by topic and audience question.
- Readable typography, accessible contrast, captions and meaningful alternative text.
- Sources, publication dates and review dates for relevant factual content.
- Transparent disclosures and a clear correction route.
- Minimal data collection and respect for privacy.

## Suggested information architecture

- Home
- Learn by topic
- Beginner guides
- StockRani
- StockRani Decodes
- About RupeeRani
- Sources and editorial standards
- Contact and corrections

Add StockRani Studio to navigation only when its scope and destination are real and maintained.
